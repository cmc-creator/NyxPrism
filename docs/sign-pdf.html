What you get

Questions

Is Sign PDF free?

Yes. Create a free NyxPrism account and use it as often as you like.

Are my files uploaded?

Your PDF is processed right in your browser — it isn't uploaded to NyxPrism.

Does it work on Mac, Windows and phones?

Yes. NyxPrism runs in any modern browser, and you can install it as an app on your computer.

How do I get someone else to sign?

Use Request Signatures to email a secure signing link to one or more people and track who has signed.
