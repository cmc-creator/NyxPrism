Privacy Policy

Effective September 22, 2026 — NyxCollective LLC

Short version: Core browser and command-line PDF tools process files locally. Cloud AI, signature requests, and document distribution transmit document content to our service or designated AI provider as described below. We store cloud-workflow documents until you delete them or the retention period expires.

1. Introduction

NyxCollective LLC ("Company", "we", "us", "our") operates NyxPrism™ (the "Software"). This Privacy Policy describes how we handle information in connection with the Software and our website at nyxprism.com.

We are committed to minimising data collection and protecting your privacy. This policy is designed to be clear and plain rather than legalistic.

2. Information We Collect

Local tools remain local. Split, merge, compress, rotate, watermark, protect, unlock, OCR, image conversion, annotation, and similar browser or command-line operations do not upload files to NyxCollective LLC unless you choose a cloud workflow.

Depending on the features you use, we collect and process:

  • Account name, email address, authentication identifiers, plan, and billing status
  • PDF files, filenames, recipient details, field values, audit events, IP addresses, and user agents used in signature or distribution workflows
  • Extracted document text and prompts submitted to cloud AI features
  • Contact-form messages and delivery metadata
  • Operational logs needed for security, reliability, and abuse prevention

3. Local Processing

All core PDF operations — split, merge, compress, rotate, watermark, protect, unlock, OCR, image conversion, and heuristic AI analysis — are executed entirely on your local machine using the libraries installed with the package. No network connection is required or initiated for these operations.

The --strategy heuristic AI mode also runs entirely locally. It uses pattern-matching and rule-based analysis without any external API calls.

4. AI Processing

The command-line package can optionally use OpenAI when you provide your own API key and select an LLM strategy. The web dashboard uses Anthropic for cloud AI features. In either case, relevant extracted text and prompts are sent to the selected provider for processing.

  • OpenAI processing is governed by OpenAI's Privacy Policy
  • Anthropic processing is governed by Anthropic's Privacy Policy
  • Do not submit content to cloud AI unless you are authorised to disclose it

5. Service Providers

We use Vercel for website hosting, Railway and PostgreSQL for API and workflow storage, Firebase for authentication and profile data, Stripe for billing, Brevo for transactional email, Google Fonts for typography, and Anthropic or OpenAI for optional AI processing. These providers process data under their own terms and privacy commitments.

6. Account Data (Professional and Enterprise Tiers)

If you create an account for a paid subscription, we will collect and store:

  • Your name and email address
  • Billing information (processed and stored by our payment processor; we do not store card numbers)
  • Account preferences and subscription status

This data is used solely to manage your subscription, provide support, and communicate service updates. We do not sell, rent, or share this data with third parties for marketing purposes.

Billing card details are handled by Stripe and are not stored by NyxCollective LLC.

7. Document Storage and Retention

Signature and distribution documents are stored in our database to provide recipient access and completed-document generation. Public links expire after 30 days. Owners can delete stored workflows from the dashboard at any time. Expired workflow records are automatically deleted 30 days after expiration. Transactional and security logs may be retained longer where reasonably necessary for fraud prevention, dispute resolution, or legal obligations.

8. Authentication and Local Storage

Firebase maintains authentication state using browser storage. NyxPrism also stores non-sensitive interface preferences and local usage counters in your browser. We do not use advertising cookies.

9. Children's Privacy

NyxPrism™ is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us immediately.

10. Data Security

We use encrypted transport, access controls, expiring high-entropy recipient links, and restricted administrative access. No system is completely secure. You should:

  • Share signature and distribution links only with their intended recipient
  • Delete cloud workflows when they are no longer needed
  • Keep NyxPrism™ updated to benefit from dependency security patches
  • Use the protect command to encrypt sensitive PDFs at rest

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will update the "Effective" date at the top of this page. For material changes that affect how we handle account data, we will notify paid subscribers via email.

12. Contact

If you have questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy, please use our contact form or email us at legal@nyxprism.com.
